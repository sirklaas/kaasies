# Brand Voice Dossier: KAASIES’ — Geen Nepkaas

---

## 1. Brand Core & Conviction

* **Target Advocate:** The real-food lover who refuses to eat tasteless, rubbery supermarket blocks—but also doesn't want to spend 45 minutes getting lectured by a pretentious cheesemonger. They want **real, top-tier artisanal cheese** without the snobbery or endless research.
* **The Enemy / Fight:** **Supermarket Rubber Cheese & Industrial Shortcuts.** Mass-produced, plastic-wrapped blocks stripped of soul, full of palm oil, age-accelerators, and zero taste.
* **Core Truth:** *"Kaas is iets nobels. Behandel het dan ook zo."* Real cheese requires time, real raw/pure milk, and devotion. Life is too short to eat boring, fake cheese.

## 4. Voice Rules & Structural Guardrails

### DO
1. **Attack the Fake, Celebrate the Real:** Be unapologetic about taking down supermarket cardboard cheese while praising small-batch makers.
2. **Write with Sensory Impact:** Talk about the crunch of salt crystals, the creaminess on the tongue, and the aroma of real maturation.
3. **Keep Curation Tight & Confident:** Don't offer 200 confusing cheeses. Offer 3 epic pieces and own them (*"Geen eindeloos schap. Alleen wat echt goed is."*).
4. **Treat the Customer Like an Insider:** Make joining the community feel like joining a secret club for real flavor.

### DON'T
1. **No Pretentious French Jargon:** Avoid snobby sommelier talk (*"hints van eikenhout en florale tonen"*). Keep it grounded and mouth-watering.
2. **Never Apologize for Being Opinionated:** If a cheese isn't up to standard, call it out.
3. **No Corporate Euphemisms:** Say *"Supermarktkaas is rubber"* instead of *"Minder hoogwaardige alternatieven"*.
